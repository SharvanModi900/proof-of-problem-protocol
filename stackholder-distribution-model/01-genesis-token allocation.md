# PoPP Genesis Token Allocation Model

A recommended stakeholder stack for launching the Proof-of-Problem Protocol (PoPP), ensuring sustainability, decentralization, and long-term alignment of incentives.

---

## 🧠 1. Inventor / Core Team

**Role:** Original visionaries, protocol designers, early builders.

**Allocation:** 15%–20% of total token supply  
**Vesting:** 4 years (1-year cliff)

**Includes:**
- Inventor (you)
- Core developers
- Protocol architects

**Why:**
- Encourages long-term commitment
- Prevents early dumping
- Signals seriousness to investors and community

---

## 💰 2. Investors

**Role:** Provide early-stage funding, advice, and network.

**Allocation:** 10%–15% of total token supply

**Sub-divisions:**
- Seed Round: 2%–5%
- Private Sale: 5%–8%
- Public Sale (IDO/ICO): 2%–3%

**Vesting:** 1–2 years with monthly unlocks after 3–6 month cliff

**Why:**
- Rewards early backers
- Provides funding without undermining decentralization

---

## 🛠️ 3. Developer Ecosystem Pool (Dev Pool / Dev DAO)

**Role:** Incentivize ongoing protocol development, bug bounties, and dApp integrations.

**Allocation:** 20%–25% of total token supply  
**Management:** Developer DAO or multisig-controlled

**Usage:**
- Hackathon prizes
- Grants
- Core development
- Security audits

**Why:**
- Keeps protocol evolving
- Opens contributions to global devs

---

## 🌐 4. Community & Validator Rewards

**Role:** Validators, users, verifiers, problem raisers/resolvers.

**Allocation:** 30%–40% of total token supply  
**Distribution:** Ongoing via PoPP mechanisms

**Usage:**
- Problem validation rewards
- Governance participation
- Staking incentives
- Community campaigns

**Why:**
- Ensures decentralization
- Rewards honest, active participation

---

## 🏛️ 5. Foundation / Treasury Reserve

**Role:** Maintain protocol stability, handle emergencies, support partnerships.

**Allocation:** 10%–15% of total token supply  
**Governance:** Controlled by DAO or foundation multisig

**Usage:**
- Legal costs
- Strategic partnerships
- Emergency reserves

**Why:**
- Provides resilience against volatility
- Supports protocol evolution

---

## 🔒 Sample Allocation Summary

| Stakeholder              | % of Token Supply | Vesting Period            |
|--------------------------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Inventor / Core Team     | 15%–20%           | 4 years (1-year cliff)    |
| Investors                | 10%–15%           | 1–2 years                 |
| Developer Pool / DAO     | 20%–25%           | Based on grants           |
| Community & Validators   | 30%–40%           | Ongoing (PoPP mechanisms) |
| Treasury / Foundation    | 10%–15%           | DAO governed              |

---

## ✅ Notes

- Transition to DAO governance over time is encouraged.
- All allocations should be transparent and auditable.
- Vesting contracts should be implemented in Solidity or Cosmos SDK depending on chain infrastructure.
