## 🔄 The Moral Reboot Protocol (MRP)

PoPP includes a **fail-safe reboot** system:  
to preserve its **original values**, not its current version.

### 🔺 Trigger Conditions (3 of 7 must match):

- Fewer than 5 active validators in 12 months  
- Memory chain silent for 3 years  
- 3+ regions in moral conflict over immutable truths  
- 90% civic reports of validator corruption  
- AI fails empathy audit  
- External civilization (e.g., AI or aliens) requests ethical translation  
- Creator signature revoked voluntarily  

### 🌀 Reboot Steps:

1. Pause all escalations  
2. Backup and fork memory chain  
3. Create **New Genesis Council**  
4. Hold **7-month cross-civilizational ethics ritual**  
5. Rebuild protocol ethos → DAO vote  
6. Publish **New Civic Charter**  
7. Release version hash as:  
   > **“Child of PoPP”**  

> Reboots do not erase history.  
> They contextualize it—  
> and birth the next ethical phase.
