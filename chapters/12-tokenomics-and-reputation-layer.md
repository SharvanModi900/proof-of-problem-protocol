# 📘 Chapter 12: Tokenomics and Reputation Layer
## 🪙 Why Incentives Matter
Protocols survive not by rules alone,
but by incentives that align with truth.

In PoPP, validators, proofers, and even problem reporters are rewarded for their honesty, effort, and impact.

This is where Tokenomics meets Trust—and Reputation becomes currency.

# 🌐 Two Main Systems:
Layer	Purpose
PoPP Token Layer	Economic rewards, payments, staking
Reputation Layer	Identity-weighted trust system for access/power

🔹 1. PoPP Token ($POPPT)
A utility token that fuels the ecosystem:

# 🔧 Use Cases:
🎯 Validator/Proofer rewards

💼 DAO governance voting power

🛠️ Access to premium features or reputation boosts

🎁 Community bounties for high-impact resolutions

🧾 Gas for protocol-level actions (proofing, appeals)

# ⚖️ Economic Design:
🔁 Circulating supply: Dynamic (via resolution rates)

📉 Burn mechanism: Token is burned on false validation

💰 Treasury pool: For validator grants, bug bounties, audit contests

💎 Staking: Validators stake tokens for validation rights

🚫 Penalty Slashing: Misbehavior → stake deducted

# 🔸 2. Reputation Layer: Trust-as-a-Score
In PoPP, your Reputation Score (R-Score) is your trust badge.
It evolves based on your behavior—not just balances.

# 📈 How You Gain Reputation:
🧪 Accurate validation work

🔍 Proving difficult or controversial problems

🧾 Submitting high-quality problem tickets

🫱🏼‍🫲🏽 Helping resolve other people's problems

📉 How You Lose Reputation:
❌ Rejecting valid problems

🪤 Colluding or validating spam

💤 Inactivity over long periods

🧩 Proof rejection by the community

🏅 R-Scores Unlock:
🎖️ Governance tiers

🧠 Access to sensitive categories (e.g., health, legal)

📊 Node visibility weight (higher R = more visibility)

🛡️ Immunity from spam detection

🧠 Why Split Rewards and Reputation?
In PoPP, money can’t buy trust.
Tokens are tradeable.
But reputation is earned, personal, and public.

# This dual-layer system prevents:

🧸 Sybil attacks

💰 Pay-for-verification abuse

🧱 Centralization of truth

🔐 Optional Reputation NFTs
High R-Score holders may be given Soulbound NFTs

These represent irrevocable badges of public trust

Cannot be sold—only earned through service
