# 📘 Chapter 27: The Open Source Manifesto

## 🔓 PoPP Must Be Open, Or It Will Be Nothing

You cannot **privatize justice**.  
You cannot **patent dignity**.  
And you cannot **restrict access to truth**.

That’s why **PoPP is 100% open-source**—  
not as a feature, but as a **founding principle**.

---

## ⚖️ The Core Belief: Code = Civic Infrastructure

The world has relied too long on **black-box systems**.

From e-governance to complaint portals to AI decisions—  
Most operate in **opaque systems** users can’t audit.

**PoPP rejects this darkness.**  
It declares:

> 🧾 “If a decision affects a life, the logic behind it must be public.”
