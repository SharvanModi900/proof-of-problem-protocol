## 🎯 3-Layer Education Strategy

### 1️⃣ Conceptual Literacy
- What is truth?  
- Why validation matters  
- How escalation works

🛠️ Tools:  
- Animated explainers  
- Comics  
- Local-language zines  
- Street plays  

### 2️⃣ System Literacy
- How to use the mobile/web app  
- How DAO voting works  
- How to trust validators  

🛠️ Tools:  
- Tutorials  
- Flowcharts  
- Simulator games  
- Fake-ticket training  

### 3️⃣ Civic Literacy
- Your right to raise issues  
- Your duty to validate  
- How this fits into democracy  

🛠️ Tools:  
- In-school curriculum  
- NGO workshops  
- PoPP awareness drives
