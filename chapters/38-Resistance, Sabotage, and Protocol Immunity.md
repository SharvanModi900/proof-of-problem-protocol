# 📘 Chapter 38: Resistance, Sabotage, and Protocol Immunity

## 🧨 The Inevitable Resistance

Systems that expose injustice will be **attacked** by injustice.

PoPP challenges:

- Political powers  
- Corrupt institutions  
- Injustice markets  
- Big data monopolies  
- Even comfort zones  

And so, it will be:

- Dismissed  
- Mocked  
- Censored  
- DDoSed  
- Legally challenged  

> This is not a **bug**.  
> This is **proof of necessity**.

## 🧪 Crisis Simulations

PoPP DAOs regularly run **“Civic Fire Drills”**:

- Fake ticket floods  
- DNS hijack drills  
- DAO polarization simulations  
- Validator exit stress tests  
- Offline-only region challenges  

> 🎮 DAO members are **scored** on response time, ethics, and solution quality.  
> Keeps the system **mentally rehearsed** for real attacks.

---

## 🧠 The Role of “Immune Nodes”

Specialized validators that act as **protocol defenders**:

- Monitor escalated risk areas  
- Run **proof-of-sabotage** oracles  
- Activate **Quarantine Mode** on data centers or DAO channels  
- Sync legal defense funds and lawyers DAO-side  

> Immune nodes are **non-voting**, **non-custodial**—but **always alert**.

---

## 📢 Handling Political Attacks

When **truth challenges power**, **power retaliates**.

PoPP responds with:

- **Mirror DAOs** in friendlier jurisdictions  
- **Transparency bombs**: escalated ticket data dumps  
- **International civic networks** to amplify the signal  
- **Validator asylum systems** for whistleblowers or banned validators
