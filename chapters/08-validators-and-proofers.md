# 📘 Chapter 8: The Role of Validators and Proofers

---

## 🧩 Who Ensures the Truth in PoPP?

In **PoPP**, validators and proofers are the **lifeblood**.  
They are not passive observers.  
They are **active agents of truth**, performing a **sacred civic duty**—  
verifying that a problem is real.

> Without validators, PoPP is just a message box.  
> With them, it becomes a **living protocol of verified reality**.

---

## 🧠 Validator vs. Proofer — What’s the Difference?

| Role      | Purpose                                        | Powers                             | Rewards                                  |
|-----------|------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Validator** | Verifies the authenticity of the problem       | Can approve/reject/flag submissions | Earn tokens or reputation                |
| **Proofer**   | Creates cryptographic proof after consensus    | Writes the problem to ledger        | Gain PoP credits + high reputation score |

> Validators **inspect**.  
> Proofers **immortalize**.

---

## 🔍 How Validators Work

Validators can be:

- 👤 Verified individuals (citizens, experts)  
- 🏛️ Institutions (NGOs, decentralized agencies)  
- 🤖 Autonomous agents (AI, IoT sensors)

They follow a **scoring system** based on:

- Reputation history  
- Domain knowledge  
- Previous accuracy  
- Geographical relevance

Multiple validators are **dynamically selected** based on the nature and location of the issue.
