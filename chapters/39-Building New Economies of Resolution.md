# 📘 Chapter 39: Building New Economies of Resolution

## 💰 The Resolution Gap

In most systems:

> Those who exploit problems get paid.  
> Those who fix them are ignored.  
> Citizens who suffer… pay the price.

PoPP **inverts** this logic:  
✅ **Solvers become the earners**  
✅ **Resolvers become the remembered**

---

## 💡 From Markets of Attention → Markets of Resolution

| Old Economy                  | PoPP Economy                                   |
|-----------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Sell ads on fake pain       | Reward validated truth fixes                   |
| Hoard data                  | Open-source civic memory                       |
| Build monopolies            | Federated DAO-based service networks           |
| Farm hype                   | Stake reputation on resolving real issues      |

> Fixing truthfully becomes **profitable, ethical, and social capital**.

---

## 🧱 Three Layers of the Resolution Economy

### 🎯 **Direct Resolution Incentives**
- Token reward for resolving a ticket  
- Bonus for quick resolution or DAO-approved solution  
- Reputation points added to solver's civic record

### 🛠 **Service Layer Opportunities**
- dApps plug into PoPP data → build services (insurance, micro-loans, legal aid)  
- Solvers offer services via **public reputation graphs**  
- DAOs can fund **local solution contractors**

### 🏅 **Resolution NFTs & Civic Proofs**
- Non-tradable badges: `"Problem Solved — July 2030"`  
- Dynamic NFTs linked to memory chain  
- Verifiable proof of impact → unlock funding, trust, DAO governance access
