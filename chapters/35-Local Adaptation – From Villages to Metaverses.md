## 🏡 Deploying PoPP in Physical Communities

### ✅ Use Case: Village Water Access

| Component       | Adaptation                                          |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| 📞 Problem Input | Via SMS or local PoPP kiosk                        |
| 🧍 Validators    | Village elders + trained youth peer groups         |
| 📄 DAO Voting    | Weekly panchayat meetings with paper ballots       |
| 🧾 Memory Chain  | Solar-powered device syncs once a day to chain     |

**Outcome:**  
A villager files a water scarcity complaint.  
→ Validated by 3 trusted peers  
→ Escalated to district DAO  
→ Issue gets solved  
→ The ticket becomes a civic story.
