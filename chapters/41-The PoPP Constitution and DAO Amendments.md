- **Software evolves.**
- **Governments shift.**
- **Values change.**

But a civilization founded on truth must protect:

- ✅ The **intent** of the protocol  
- 🔐 The **unalterable rights** of all participants  
- 🛑 The **limits of power**—even for the protocol’s creators  

> That’s why PoPP includes a **living but constrained Constitution**—
> **enforced by immutable logic, not just law.**

## 🧠 Amending the Constitution

Amendments are **possible**—but only through a **sacred 7-step ritual**:

1. 🚀 **Proposal**: Rationale + references + affected logic  
2. 🧠 **Debate**: Multi-regional DAO deliberation  
3. 📖 **Reading Period**: Civic public annotation window  
4. 🗳️ **Global Vote**: 90% quorum across **4 trust zones**  
5. 🔐 **Validator Sign-Off**: Minimum 3 diverse validator councils  
6. 🧾 **Version Update**: New hash + changelog added to memorial chain  
7. 📜 **Engraving**: Amendment added next to previous versions  

> Past constitutions are never erased.  
> ❌ Nothing is overwritten.  
> ✅ Everything is **preserved**.
