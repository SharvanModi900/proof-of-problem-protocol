# 📘 Chapter 10: Escalation Mechanism and Truth Flow

---

## 🔺 The Core Idea: Truth Must Move

In most systems, problems get stuck.  
Tickets are closed without action.  
Issues stay buried in departments or forgotten in email threads.

**PoPP** is designed to **move truth**—from awareness to resolution.  
This movement is called the **Truth Flow**.

---

## 🔁 Escalation Is Not a Threat—It’s a Lifeline

In PoPP, escalation means:

- The problem could not be resolved at the local level  
- So it now rises **automatically** to the next responsible layer

It eliminates **waiting, blame games, and political delay**.

---

## 🧱 3 Levels of Escalation in PoPP

### 1. 🏘️ Local Layer

- Community DAOs  
- Municipal bodies  
- Peer validator groups  

They try to resolve the problem.  
If ignored or falsely closed, it **escalates**.

### 2. 🏙️ Regional Layer

- State governments  
- Zonal admins  
- Organizational branches  

A second validator round confirms the status.  
A **response timer** is triggered.

### 3. 🌐 Global/Public Layer

The problem is made **fully public** and **immutable**, triggering:

- Journalistic attention  
- Civic pressure  
- Automated smart contract actions  
  - Fund lock  
  - Admin alerts  

---

Problem Reported

---

## 🤖 Smart Escalation Logic

PoPP uses:

- **Time-based triggers**  
  - (If no action within 72 hours, escalate)
- **Reputation-based routing**  
  - (Low-trust nodes skipped at higher layers)
- **Severity auto-ranking**  
  - (Emergency = instant top-layer broadcast)
- **AI-assisted tagging**  
  - (For intelligent categorization and routing)
